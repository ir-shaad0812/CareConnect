## 1. Introduction

### What is CareConnect? 🌟

CareConnect is a trusted care booking platform that bridges the gap between **families who need care** and **professional caregivers** who provide it. Whether you're looking for elderly care, child care, disability support, or specialized medical assistance, CareConnect provides a safe, transparent, and regulated environment for booking and managing care services.

### How CareConnect Works

CareConnect operates on a **structured, multi-step workflow** designed to protect both families and caregivers. Unlike simple marketplace apps, CareConnect enforces a formal agreement process, compliance tracking, and escrow-style payments — ensuring that every booking is backed by mutual accountability.

```
👨‍👩‍👧 Care Seeker                    👩‍⚕️ Caregiver
     │                                    │
     │  1. Browse & Book ──────────────►  │
     │                                    │  2. Accept / Reject
     │  ◄──────────────── 3. Accepted     │
     │                                    │
     │  4. Both Accept Agreement ◄──────► │
     │                                    │
     │  5. Care Seeker Pays               │
     │                                    │
     │  ◄──────── 6. Service Begins ────► │
     │                                    │
     │  7. Caregiver Submits Daily Logs   │
     │                                    │
     │  ◄──────── 8. Service Completed ── │
     │                                    │
     │  9. Review & Rate ─────────────►  │
```

### Key Platform Principles

| Principle | What It Means |
|-----------|---------------|
| 🔒 **Mutual Agreement** | Both parties must formally accept the service agreement before payment |
| 💳 **Protected Payments** | Payments are only enabled after full agreement acceptance |
| 📋 **Daily Accountability** | Caregivers must submit daily tracking logs during active bookings |
| 🛡️ **Admin Oversight** | Admins monitor compliance, resolve disputes, and enforce platform rules |
| ⭐ **Verified Reviews** | Only completed bookings can receive reviews |

### Platform Roles at a Glance

| Role | Primary Function |
|------|-----------------|
| 👨‍👩‍👧 **Care Seeker** | Books caregivers, manages bookings, pays for services |
| 👩‍⚕️ **Caregiver** | Provides care, accepts bookings, submits daily logs |
| 🔧 **Admin** | Monitors platform, enforces compliance, resolves disputes |

## 2. Getting Started

### 2.1 Creating Your Account

CareConnect supports two registration methods:

1. **Email & Password Registration**
2. **Google Sign-In** (one-click, no password required)

#### Step-by-Step Registration

**Step 1:** Open the CareConnect app or visit the web platform.

**Step 2:** Click **"Get Started"** or **"Sign Up"**.

**Step 3:** Choose your sign-up method:
- Enter your email address and create a strong password, **OR**

- 👩‍⚕️ **I provide care services** → Caregiver

## 3. Care Seeker Guide

### 3.1 Setting Up Your Profile

A complete profile increases caregiver response rates and ensures accurate service matching.

#### Complete Your Care Recipient Profile

Navigate to **Dashboard → Profile → Care Recipients** and add details for each person you're booking care for:

- **Name** of the care recipient
- **Age** and **gender**
- **Type of care needed** (elderly, pediatric, disability, post-surgical, etc.)
- **Medical conditions** or special needs
- **Mobility level** and physical requirements
- **Dietary restrictions**
- **Preferred language**
- **Additional notes** for caregivers

> 🔒 This information is shared only with the caregivers you book and is protected under our privacy policy.

#### Add Emergency Contacts

Go to **Profile → Emergency Contacts** and add at least one emergency contact. This person can be contacted by the caregiver or platform in case of an emergency during a care session.

### 3.2 Browsing Caregivers

#### Using the Search & Filter System

From your dashboard, click **"Find a Caregiver"** to access the search page.

**Available Filters:**

| Filter | Options |
|--------|---------|
| 🗓️ Service Dates | Start date, end date |
| ⏰ Availability | Morning, afternoon, evening, overnight |
| 💰 Price Range | Min/max hourly rate |
| 📍 Location | Distance from your address |
| 🌟 Rating | Minimum star rating |
| 🧑‍⚕️ Service Type | Elderly, pediatric, disability, etc. |
| 🗣️ Languages | Preferred language |

#### Reading a Caregiver Profile

Each caregiver profile contains:

- **Profile photo** and verification badges
- **Bio** and care philosophy
- **Service types** offered
- **Experience** (years + previous employers)
- **Qualifications** and certifications

### 3.3 Understanding Caregiver Availability

Caregivers set their availability in **slots** — specific days and times when they can accept bookings. When browsing, you will see:

- 🟢 **Available** — Caregiver is free on this date/time
- 🔴 **Unavailable** — Slot is blocked or already booked
- 🟡 **Pending** — Another booking request is under review

> ⚠️ Availability shown is real-time. Slots can be taken by other care seekers at any moment. Complete your booking promptly after finding an available caregiver.

### 3.4 Booking a Caregiver — Step by Step

#### Step 1: Select a Caregiver

From search results or a caregiver's profile, click **"Book Now"**.

#### Step 2: Fill in Booking Details

| Field | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| Care Recipient | Select who the care is for |
| Service Type | Choose the type of care needed |
| Start Date & Time | When the service begins |
| End Date & Time | When the service ends |
| Booking Type | Hourly / Daily / Weekly / Monthly |
| Location | Where the care will be provided |
| Special Instructions | Any notes for the caregiver |

#### Step 3: Review the Pricing Summary

Before submitting, review the cost breakdown:

```
Rate:           NPR 500 / hr
Total Hours:    8 hrs
──────────────────────────
Subtotal:       NPR 4,000
Platform Fee:   NPR 400  (10%)
──────────────────────────
Total Due:      NPR 4,400
```

#### Step 4: Submit Your Booking Request

Click **"Send Booking Request"**. Your request is now in **Submitted** status. The caregiver will be notified immediately.

> ⏳ Caregivers typically respond within a few hours. You will receive a notification when they accept or decline.

#### Step 5: Wait for Caregiver Acceptance

- If the caregiver **accepts** → Booking moves to **Accepted** status and an Agreement is generated.
- If the caregiver **declines** → You will be notified with a reason (if provided). You can then search for another caregiver.

#### Step 6: Review and Accept the Agreement

Once the caregiver accepts your booking, a formal service agreement is automatically generated. You **must** review and accept this agreement before payment becomes available.

See [Section 5: Agreement Guide](#5-agreement-guide) for full details on your obligations.

To accept:
1. Go to **Dashboard → My Bookings → [Your Booking]**
2. Scroll to the **Agreement Panel**
3. Read the agreement carefully
4. Click **"Accept Agreement"**

> ✅ After both you and the caregiver have accepted, the booking moves to **Payment** status and payment is enabled.

#### Step 7: Make Payment

Once both parties have accepted the agreement, a **"Make Payment"** button appears. See [Section 9: Payment Guide](#9-payment-guide) for full payment instructions.

#### Step 8: Booking Confirmed! 🎉

After successful payment, your booking status changes to **Confirmed**. The caregiver will check in when the care session begins.

### 3.8 Reviewing Daily Logs from the Caregiver

Caregivers are required to submit a **daily tracking log** for every active booking day. As a care seeker, you should:

1. Go to **Dashboard → My Bookings → [Booking] → Tracking Logs**
2. Review each daily log entry submitted by the caregiver
3. Logs include:
   - Activities performed
   - Meals prepared and eaten
   - Medications administered
   - Mood and behavioral notes
   - Any incidents or concerns
   - Proof-of-work photos (if uploaded)
4. Acknowledge each log once reviewed

> 📌 If a log contains concerning information or you disagree with its contents, use the in-app chat to communicate with the caregiver or raise a dispute.

### 3.9 Raising a Dispute

If you have a serious concern about the service quality, a missed obligation, or a payment issue, you can raise a formal dispute:

1. Go to **Dashboard → My Bookings → [Booking]**
2. Click **"Raise a Dispute"**
3. Select the dispute category:
   - 🚫 Service not delivered as agreed
   - ⏰ Caregiver did not show up
   - 💔 Unprofessional conduct
   - 💰 Payment or billing issue
   - 📋 Inaccurate tracking logs
   - 🔒 Other
4. Provide a detailed description
5. Upload supporting evidence (photos, screenshots, messages)
6. Click **"Submit Dispute"**

An admin will review your dispute within **2–5 business days** and notify you of the outcome.

> ⚠️ **Important:** Keep all booking-related communication on the CareConnect platform. Off-platform messages cannot be used as evidence in disputes.

| Tuesday | ✅ | 08:00 AM – 06:00 PM |

### 4.3 Receiving and Accepting Booking Requests

When a care seeker sends you a booking request, you will receive:
- An **in-app notification** 🔔
- An **email notification** 📧
- A **dashboard alert** on your Caregiver Dashboard

#### Responding to a Booking Request

1. Go to **Dashboard → Booking Requests**
2. Click on the pending request to view full details:
   - Care seeker information
   - Care recipient details (age, needs, conditions)
   - Requested dates and times
   - Location
   - Service type
   - Proposed rate and total
3. Review all details carefully
4. Choose your response:
   - ✅ **Accept** — You agree to take the booking
   - ❌ **Decline** — You are unable to take the booking (provide a reason)

> ⏰ **Respond within 24 hours.** Unanswered requests may be automatically declined and may negatively affect your response rate.

#### What Happens After You Accept

1. The booking moves from **Submitted** → **Accepted** status
2. A **Service Agreement** is automatically generated
3. Both you and the care seeker must accept the agreement
4. Once both parties accept → payment is enabled for the care seeker
5. After payment → booking becomes **Confirmed**
6. On the service start date → you check in to make the booking **Active**

> 🚨 **Critical:** You must accept the **booking request** before the agreement is generated. If you try to accept the agreement without first accepting the booking request, you will receive a 400 error. Always accept the booking request FIRST.

### 4.4 Understanding the Agreement and Your 8 Obligations

| 8 | ✅ **Platform Compliance** | Conduct all bookings, payments, and communications through the CareConnect platform only |

> 📄 The agreement can be downloaded as a PDF or printed using the buttons in the Agreement Panel. Keep a copy for your records.

### 4.5 Active Bookings: Check-In and Check-Out

#### Checking In

When you arrive at the care location at the start of a session:

1. Open CareConnect on your device
2. Go to **Dashboard → Active Bookings → [Booking]**
3. Click **"Check In"**
4. Confirm your location (GPS may be recorded)
5. Your check-in time is logged and the care seeker is notified

> ✅ Check-in officially starts the **Active** phase of the booking.

#### Checking Out

At the end of the care session:

1. Go to **Dashboard → Active Bookings → [Booking]**
2. Click **"Check Out"**
3. Add any final notes about the session
4. Confirm check-out

> 📌 The platform calculates total hours worked based on check-in and check-out times. Ensure accuracy before confirming check-out.

#### Multi-Day Bookings

For bookings spanning multiple days:
- Check in at the start of each day's session
- Check out at the end of each day's session
- Submit a daily log before your check-out each day

### 4.6 Submitting Daily Tracking Logs (Mandatory)

Daily tracking log submission is **mandatory** for all active bookings. Failure to submit logs is a compliance violation and can result in account suspension.

#### How to Submit a Daily Log

1. Go to **Dashboard → Active Bookings → [Booking] → Daily Logs**
2. Click **"Submit Today's Log"**
3. Fill in all required fields:

| Field | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| 📅 Date | Auto-populated with today's date |
| ⏰ Session Duration | Start and end time of care |
| 🍽️ Meals | What the care recipient ate (breakfast, lunch, dinner, snacks) |
| 💊 Medications | Medications administered, dosages, times |
| 🏃 Activities | Physical activities, exercises, or therapies completed |
| 😊 Mood & Behavior | How the care recipient was feeling and behaving |
| 🏥 Health Observations | Any changes in health, pain levels, or symptoms |
| 🚨 Incidents | Any accidents, falls, or unusual events (mandatory if occurred) |
| 📝 Additional Notes | Anything else the family should know |

4. Upload **proof-of-work photos** (see Section 4.7)
5. Click **"Submit Log"**

> ⏰ **Deadline:** Daily logs must be submitted by **11:59 PM** on the same day of service. Late or missing logs are flagged by the compliance system.

---

### 4.7 Uploading Proof-of-Work Photos

To provide transparency and evidence of care delivery, you can (and for some booking types must) upload photos with your daily log.

#### What to Photo (Appropriate Examples)

- ✅ Meals prepared for the care recipient
- ✅ Activities or exercises done together
- ✅ Completed tasks (cleaned spaces, organized medications)
- ✅ Completed therapy exercises or mobility activities
- ✅ General wellbeing shots (with care recipient's consent)

#### What NOT to Photo

- ❌ Intimate or personal care moments
- ❌ Anything that could compromise the care recipient's dignity
- ❌ Private areas of the home unrelated to care

#### How to Upload Photos

In the Daily Log form, scroll to **"Proof of Work"** and:
1. Click **"Add Photos"**
2. Select up to **10 photos** per log entry
3. Add a brief caption for each photo
4. Photos are uploaded securely and visible only to the care seeker and admin

### 4.8 Understanding Tracking Compliance

CareConnect uses an automated compliance monitoring system. Here's what you need to know:

| Compliance Item | Requirement | Consequence of Failure |
|----------------|-------------|----------------------|
| Daily Log Submission | Every active day | Warning → Strike → Suspension |
| Check-In Time | Within 30 min of scheduled start | Flagged as late |
| Check-Out Confirmation | Before midnight | Log marked incomplete |
| Incident Reporting | Same day as incident | Compliance violation |
| Photo Evidence | Per booking type | Log flagged as unverified |

#### Compliance Score

Your compliance score is visible on your dashboard and affects:
- Your ranking in search results
- Your eligibility for **Featured Caregiver** status
- Your account standing

> 💡 **Tip:** Submit your daily log immediately after the session ends — don't wait until late at night. This keeps your compliance score high.

## 5. Agreement Guide

### 5.1 What is the CareConnect Service Agreement?

The Service Agreement is a **binding digital contract** generated automatically when a caregiver accepts a booking request. It formalizes the responsibilities of both parties and is a prerequisite for payment.

Every agreement has:
- A **unique Agreement ID** (e.g., `AGR-CC2026041414AL`)
- The full names of both the care seeker and caregiver
- The booking details (dates, service type, location)
- The payment summary
- The obligations of both parties

---

### 5.2 The Agreement Workflow

```
Caregiver Accepts Booking Request
            ↓
  Agreement Auto-Generated
            ↓
  ┌─────────────────────┐
  │  Care Seeker:       │
  │  ⏳ Awaiting...     │     ← Both parties see this panel
  │                     │
  │  Caregiver:         │
  │  ⏳ Awaiting...     │
  └─────────────────────┘
            ↓
  Both click "Accept Agreement"
  (in any order — each action is independent)
            ↓
  ┌─────────────────────┐
  │  Care Seeker: ✓     │
  │  Caregiver:   ✓     │
  └─────────────────────┘
            ↓
     Payment Enabled 💳
```

---

### 5.3 For Care Seekers: How to Accept the Agreement

1. Navigate to **Dashboard → My Bookings**
2. Find the booking with **"Agreement"** status
3. Click on the booking to open its details
4. Scroll down to the **Agreement Panel**
5. Read through the full agreement terms
6. Click the **"Accept Agreement"** button
7. A confirmation message will appear: *"You have accepted the agreement"*

> 🔄 Acceptance is **idempotent** — clicking "Accept" multiple times has no additional effect. You can safely click it again if you're unsure it was registered.

#### After You Accept

Your name in the Agreement Panel will show **"Accepted ✓"** in green. If the caregiver has also accepted, the payment button will immediately become active. If the caregiver has not yet accepted, you will see their status as **"Awaiting acceptance"** and must wait.

---

### 5.4 For Caregivers: How to Accept the Agreement

1. Navigate to **Dashboard → My Bookings**

## 11. Troubleshooting

### 11.1 "Agreement Not Showing"

**Symptom:** You accepted or received a booking, but the Agreement Panel is not visible.

**Possible Causes & Solutions:**

| Cause | Solution |
|-------|---------|
| Caregiver has not yet accepted the booking REQUEST | Wait for the caregiver to accept the request first — the agreement only generates after acceptance |
| Browser/app cache issue | Hard-refresh the page (Ctrl+Shift+R) or force-close and reopen the app |
| Slow network / sync delay | Wait 1–2 minutes and refresh again |
| Booking is in wrong status | Check the booking status — agreement only appears at "Accepted" stage or later |
| Account session expired | Log out and log back in |

> 💡 The agreement is generated **only after the caregiver accepts the booking request**. If the booking is still in "Submitted" status, the caregiver has not responded yet.

---

### 11.2 "400 Error When Accepting Agreement"

**Symptom:** You click "Accept Agreement" and receive a `400 Bad Request` error.

**Root Cause:** This error almost always means the **caregiver has not accepted the booking REQUEST** first. The agreement acceptance endpoint requires the booking to be in the correct state, which depends on the caregiver having formally confirmed the booking.

**The correct sequence is:**

```
Step 1: Care seeker submits booking request
Step 2: CAREGIVER accepts the BOOKING REQUEST  ← (This must happen first!)
Step 3: Agreement is generated automatically
Step 4: Both parties accept the AGREEMENT
Step 5: Payment is enabled
```

**If you are a Caregiver seeing this error:**
1. Go to **Dashboard → Booking Requests**
2. Find the booking and confirm it shows "Accepted" status
3. If it still shows "Pending", click **"Accept Booking"** first
4. Then return to the agreement and click "Accept Agreement"

**If you are a Care Seeker seeing this error:**
- Notify your caregiver that they need to accept the booking request before the agreement can be processed
- Use the in-app chat to communicate this

**If the error persists after following the correct sequence:**
- Note your booking ID and the exact error message
- Contact support with these details

---

### 11.3 "Booking Stuck in Pending / Submitted"

**Symptom:** Your booking has been in "Submitted" status for more than 24 hours with no caregiver response.

**Solutions:**

1. **Check notifications** — The caregiver may have responded, but you missed the notification
2. **Refresh your dashboard** — Pull to refresh or hard-refresh the browser
3. **Check the caregiver's profile** — They may have updated their availability or become unavailable
4. **Send a chat message** — Use the in-app chat to gently remind the caregiver
5. **Cancel and rebook** — If more than 48 hours pass with no response, cancel the request and find another caregiver
6. **Contact support** — If you cannot cancel, support can assist

> ⚠️ Most caregivers respond within a few hours. If a caregiver consistently fails to respond to requests, this affects their response rate score.

---

### 11.4 "Tracking Log Not Submitting"

**Symptom:** You try to submit a daily log and it either doesn't go through or shows an error.

**Possible Causes & Solutions:**

| Cause | Solution |
|-------|---------|
| Booking is not in "Active" status | You can only submit logs when the booking is Active — check the booking status |
| Missing required fields | Scroll through the log form and complete all required fields (marked with *) |
| Photos too large | Compress photos before uploading (each photo should be under 10MB) |
| Network connectivity | Check your internet connection; try switching between Wi-Fi and mobile data |
| Session expired | Log out and log back in, then try submitting again |
| Date mismatch | Ensure the log date matches today's date; you cannot submit logs for future dates |
| Already submitted today | Check if a log was already submitted for today — you cannot submit twice for the same day |

**If the issue persists:**
1. Take a screenshot of the error message
2. Note the booking ID and the date of the log
3. Contact support — they can manually verify and resolve log submission issues

---

### 11.5 "Payment Button Not Appearing"

**Symptom:** Both parties seem to have accepted the agreement, but the payment button is not visible.

**Checklist:**

- [ ] Verify YOUR acceptance is confirmed (look for "Accepted ✓" next to your name in the Agreement Panel)
- [ ] Verify the CAREGIVER'S acceptance is confirmed (their name should show "Accepted ✓")
- [ ] Refresh the page / restart the app
- [ ] Check the booking status — it should be "Payment" stage
- [ ] Ensure the booking hasn't been cancelled or expired
- [ ] Check if you have a valid payment method added to your account (**Profile → Payment Methods**)

**If all above are confirmed and button still doesn't appear:**
- Contact support with your booking ID

## 12. Frequently Asked Questions

### General Questions

**Q: Is CareConnect available in my area?**
A: CareConnect is currently available in Nepal with plans to expand. Use the location filter in search to find caregivers in your area. If no caregivers are listed in your area, check back soon or contact support to express interest.

**Q: How do I know a caregiver is trustworthy?**
A: All caregivers on CareConnect must pass identity verification, document verification, and a background check before their profile is activated. Look for the ✅ **Verified** and 🔍 **Background Checked** badges on their profile. You can also read verified reviews from past clients.

**Q: Can I book a caregiver for recurring services?**
A: Yes! When creating a booking, select the **"Recurring"** booking type and set the frequency (daily, weekly, monthly) and end date. Each recurrence follows the same agreement and payment process.

**Q: What happens if the caregiver cancels on short notice?**
A: If a caregiver cancels a confirmed booking, you receive a **full refund**. The caregiver's cancellation rate is recorded and visible on their profile. You can then re-book with another caregiver.

---

### Agreement Questions

**Q: Do I have to accept the agreement, or can I skip it?**
A: No — the agreement is mandatory. Payment cannot be initiated until **both** the care seeker and caregiver have formally accepted the agreement. This is a platform requirement that protects both parties.

**Q: Can I negotiate the terms of the agreement?**
A: The core agreement terms are standardized by CareConnect. However, you can add specific care instructions, special requirements, and notes during booking creation. These become part of the service record.

**Q: What happens if one party refuses to accept the agreement?**
A: If either party refuses to accept the agreement within a reasonable timeframe, the booking can be cancelled without penalty. Contact support if you believe the other party is delaying in bad faith.

**Q: Is the agreement legally binding?**
A: The CareConnect Service Agreement is a digital contract between the two parties. While CareConnect facilitates and enforces it within the platform, its legal standing depends on your local jurisdiction. Consult a legal professional if you have specific legal concerns.

### Payment Questions

**Q: Why is there a 10% platform fee?**
A: The 10% platform fee covers secure payment processing, background check infrastructure, compliance monitoring, 24/7 support, insurance, and ongoing platform development. This fee ensures the safety and reliability of the platform for everyone.

**Q: Can I pay the caregiver directly (outside the platform)?**
A: No. Off-platform payments are a violation of the CareConnect Service Agreement and forfeit all platform protections, including dispute resolution, insurance coverage, and payment security. Off-platform arrangements can also result in account suspension for both parties.

**Q: What if my payment fails?**
A: If a payment fails, check that: (1) your card/wallet has sufficient funds, (2) your payment method details are correct, (3) your bank hasn't blocked the transaction. Try a different payment method if the issue persists. Contact support if all methods fail.

**Q: When does the caregiver receive their money?**
A: Caregiver earnings are released after the booking is marked **Completed**. The net amount (after 10% platform fee) is transferred to the caregiver's registered bank account. Processing time depends on the bank (usually 1–5 business days).

### Tracking & Compliance Questions

**Q: What if the caregiver doesn't submit a daily log?**
A: You will receive a notification if your caregiver misses a log submission deadline. You can report this through the platform, and admins will follow up with the caregiver. Repeated missed logs can be grounds for a dispute.

**Q: Can I see the caregiver's log before they submit it?**
A: No. Logs are submitted by the caregiver and become visible to you immediately upon submission. You cannot view a log that hasn't been submitted yet.

**Q: Do I need to acknowledge daily logs?**
A: While acknowledgment is recommended, it's not mandatory. However, unacknowledged logs mean you are considered to have received the information — you cannot later claim you were unaware of something documented in a log.

**Q: What counts as an "incident" in the daily log?**
A: An incident is any unexpected event that affected the care recipient's safety, health, or wellbeing. Examples include: falls, medication errors, allergic reactions, behavioral episodes, property damage, or any situation requiring emergency services.

### Dispute Questions

**Q: How long do I have to raise a dispute?**
A: Disputes should be raised as soon as possible after the issue occurs. For most issues, you have up to **7 days after booking completion** to raise a formal dispute. Time-sensitive incidents should be reported immediately.

**Q: What evidence should I provide in a dispute?**
A: Include: screenshots of relevant chat messages, photos, the daily log entries in question, specific booking dates and times, and a clear description of what was agreed vs. what was delivered. The more evidence you provide, the faster the resolution.

**Q: Can I dispute a payment after it's been released?**
A: Payment disputes can be raised up to **7 days after booking completion**. After funds have been released to the caregiver and the dispute window has passed, reversal may not be possible. Act quickly if you have concerns.

### Account Questions

**Q: Can I have both a Care Seeker and Caregiver account?**
A: No. Each account is assigned a single role during registration. If you need to use both roles, you would need to create a second account with a different email address.

**Q: How do I change my email or password?**
A: Go to **Account Settings → Security** to update your email address or password. Email changes require verification of the new email.

**Q: My account was suspended — what do I do?**
A: You will receive an email explaining the reason for suspension. Follow the instructions in that email or contact support at the address in [Section 13](#13-contact-support) to appeal the decision.

**Q: How do I delete my account?**
A: Go to **Account Settings → Privacy → Delete Account**. Note that account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Any pending bookings must be resolved before deletion is possible.
